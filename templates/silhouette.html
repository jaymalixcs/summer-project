📉 Silhouette Score

A quantitative measure of clustering quality — validating our choice of K=5.

❓ What is the Silhouette Score?

The Silhouette Score measures how similar each customer is to its own cluster compared to other clusters. It ranges from -1 (bad) to +1 (perfect).

s(i) = (b − a) / max(a, b)
a = mean intra-cluster distance  ·  b = mean nearest-cluster distance
s(i) > 0
Well clustered
s(i) < 0
Misclassified
